## home.jpg

A lone silhouetted figure, small and featureless, stands before an immense swirling opening in a vast and impossible sky. The sky is the entire subject — deep warm violet and rich plum clouds that glow softly from within, curling and billowing in painterly formations, lit from behind by a source of soft warm lavender light that bleeds through in diffused layers. The center of the opening glows with pale warm lavender fading to soft lilac — not white, not cold, but warm. The surrounding clouds are deep purple-violet and plum, not blue or indigo. The warmth stays consistent throughout: even the darkest regions of the sky lean purple and violet rather than blue or grey. Stars are faintly visible in the deepest plum regions above. The figure is pure silhouette at the bottom quarter of the frame, no detail, just shape — dwarfed by the scale of the sky. The ground beneath them is minimal and abstract, almost absent. Colors dissolve into each other at every edge. The mood is warm, vast, and dreamlike. Nothing is sharp. Nothing is cold.

## projects.jpg

The dock is the only thing in this world that was built by human hands — everything else is atmosphere and sky. The figure stands at the very tip, upright, looking out into the mist ahead. The sky above is a deep gradient: midnight indigo and violet at the top, transitioning through deep blue into a warm amber-gold glow at the horizon where the mist is thickest. Stars are faintly visible in the indigo above. The still water below mirrors the sky's gradient softly, with no sharp reflections. A soft diffuse mist wraps the entire scene, softening every edge and dissolving the far end of the dock into the fog. Nothing is sharp. Nothing is fully resolved. The figure is pure silhouette — no facial features, no clothing detail.
